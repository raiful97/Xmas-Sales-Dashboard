# Xmas Gift Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing Christmas gift sales performance across seasons, countries, customer segments, shopping channels, products, payment methods, and shopping behavior.

## Project Overview

This project explores Christmas gift sales across the seasons shown in the dashboard, from **2018/2019 to 2021/2022**. It brings key sales, quantity, and profit metrics into a two-page report to help users compare performance and explore customer purchasing patterns.

## Dashboard Highlights

### Page 1: Sales Overview
- Latest-season sales, quantity sold, and profit KPIs
- Comparison with the previous season
- Sales performance by country
- Customer segmentation by age and gender
- Shopping channel comparison: In-store, Online, and Xmas Market
- Payment-method patterns by customer segment
- Best-selling product categories, including Toys, Clothing, Accessories, Books, and Building Sets

### Page 2: Customer & Shopping Behavior
- Relationship between average unit price and quantity sold
- Monthly quantity sold by shopping channel
- Sales by shopping channel across November, December, and January
- Quantity sold by Christmas budget category
- Common units per order
- Shopping activity by day of the week, gender, and time of day

## Key Metrics Shown

| Metric | Latest Season | Previous Season |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sales | €7.09M | €6.95M |
| Quantity Sold | 42.4K | 41.5K |
| Profit | €5.45M | €5.35M |

The dashboard reports approximately **1.9% sales growth**, **2.0% quantity growth**, and **1.9% profit growth** compared with the previous season.

- How did sales, quantity sold, and profit change compared with the previous season?
- Which countries performed best?
- Which customer age and gender segments contributed to sales?
- Where did customers shop: in-store, online, or at the Christmas market?
- Which product categories were best sellers?
- How did price and budget relate to quantity sold?
- When did customers shop, by month, day, and time?
